## Mission

> Bridge the opportunity gap faced by low-income BIPOC youth and adults through entrepreneurship, technology, workforce development, and education.

**Core themes:** Economic mobility — generational wealth — entrepreneurship — education — technology — AI — workforce development — climate justice — community ownership — social innovation — BIPOC empowerment

---

## Vision

Create publicly accessible educational ecosystems where underserved communities gain access to:
- Entrepreneurial education
- AI education and coding
- Green workforce training
- Mentors, investment, internships, and employment
- Community ownership

Long-term goal: expand nationally and internationally.

## Programs

### Journey Platform
Digital learning platform with: gamification, multimedia learning, AI integration, project-based learning, assessments, customizable curriculum, instructor dashboard, self-paced learning, badges, rewards, certifications.
Used for: entrepreneurship, coding, AI, workforce development, solar.

### Startup NYCHA
- **Audience:** NYCHA residents
- **Format:** Six-month accelerator
- **Components:** Mentorship, business coaching, customer discovery, Lean Startup, business models, pitching, seed funding, networking
- **Outputs:** Businesses launched, community ventures, pitch competition

### Cambio Solar
- Solar workforce development
- Certifications: OSHA, GPRO
- Internships, green entrepreneurship, cooperative development, community solar
- Focus: South Bronx, NYCHA, environmental justice

### Social Entrepreneurship Incubator
- 12-week accelerator
- Curriculum: Design Thinking, systems thinking, Lean Startup, customer discovery, business models, pitching

### Coding & AI
- Curriculum: Python, HTML, C++, AI tools, prompt engineering, software development, digital products
