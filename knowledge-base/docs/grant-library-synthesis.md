## Overall Organizational Background

Cambio Labs is a New York City-based nonprofit founded in 2021 by Sebastián Martín to address systemic barriers facing low-income and BIPOC youth and adults. The organization combines entrepreneurship education, workforce development, educational technology, AI, coding, and clean energy training to help participants build careers, launch businesses, and create community wealth. The organization emphasizes co-designing programs with communities, especially NYCHA residents, so participants become creators of solutions rather than recipients of services.

## Overall Narrative Summary

**Mission:** Expand economic mobility by empowering underserved communities through entrepreneurship, technology, and workforce development.

**Problem:** Systemic inequities limit access to education, digital skills, capital, and quality employment for low-income and BIPOC communities.

**Solution:** Deliver community-designed, technology-enabled programs — entrepreneurship accelerators, AI and coding education, the Journey platform, and clean energy workforce training.

**Impact:** Increase employment, business creation, wealth-building, digital literacy, and community ownership while advancing climate and economic justice.

**Vision:** Build a scalable national ecosystem of accessible entrepreneurship and workforce education that helps communities create lasting economic opportunity.
