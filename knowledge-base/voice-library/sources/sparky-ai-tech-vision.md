# Sparky AI — tech vision

## What this source is

A ~2-minute pitch introducing Sparky, Cambio Labs' AI learning assistant, to a tech-for-good sponsor
audience. It's the shortest source in the library — a compressed problem/solution/ask structure with
almost no slack in it, which makes it the best reference for tight product or fundraising copy that
has to do a lot in very little space.

## How the voice works

- **Names the org's whole mission in one sentence before naming the product.** "Cambio Labs
  economically empowers low-income youth, workers, and social entrepreneurs through educational
  courses and learning technology." Product gets introduced only after the mission does — never the
  reverse.
- **States two audiences for the same platform in one breath.** Journey is for Cambio's own courses
  *and* for "other educators and course creators" — the platform-not-just-a-program framing shows up
  immediately, not as a later pivot.
- **Problem stats arrive in pairs, back to back, no adjectives.** "54% of Gen Z K–12 students say
  school just isn't engaging. Two million students drop out every year, and low-income students are
  five times more likely to drop out." The numbers alone carry the urgency.
- **States the problem on both sides of the classroom, not just the beneficiary side.** Student
  disengagement gets one stat; teacher burnout gets its own: "55% of teachers say they are considering
  leaving the profession earlier than planned." A product pitch that only names the beneficiary's
  problem is missing half the audience it needs to win over.
- **The solution sentence names exactly what the tech does, as a list of verbs, tied straight back to
  the two problems just stated:** "AI-powered, gamified, and scalable learning technology that can
  engage students while supporting overworked teachers." No abstraction between problem and feature.
- **States development stage plainly, without inflating it.** "has already created an MVP... and is
  preparing to test it with real learners" — not "revolutionary AI platform," an honest maturity
  marker. Direct evidence of the audit's Rule 03 (name gaps/status plainly) showing up in a spoken
  pitch, not just written material.
- **The ask is a list of named roles, not a vibe.** "coders, data scientists, AI experts, LLM
  trainers, and corporate collaborators" — someone in the audience can self-select immediately.
- **Closes on a single crafted line that ties the product name back to the assistant's function** —
  the one moment of wordplay in an otherwise plain-spoken piece, used exactly once, at the very end.

## Characteristic excerpts

**Mission before product**

> Cambio Labs economically empowers low-income youth, workers, and social entrepreneurs through
> educational courses and learning technology. We created the Journey platform to deliver and scale
> our courses, and to enable other educators and course creators to deliver and scale theirs.

**Problem, stated in bare paired statistics — both sides of the classroom**

> In the U.S., 54% of Gen Z K–12 students say school just isn't engaging. Two million students drop
> out every year, and low-income students are five times more likely to drop out.
>
> At the same time, 55% of teachers say they are considering leaving the profession earlier than
> planned because of burnout and administrative workload.

**Global-scale framing, still bare**

> There is also an urgent need to reach 250 million out-of-school children and 400 million
> underemployed adults around the world—underserved learners who could benefit from access to
> educational and workforce training, along with personalized coaching.

**Solution sentence — features as verbs, directly answering the stated problem**

> That's why we're building Sparky, our AI assistant designed to personalize and credentialize
> learning for students at scale while helping teachers and program administrators with curriculum
> development, instructional coaching, assessment, and reporting.

**Stage disclosure — candid, not inflated**

> Cambio Labs' small but mighty team has already created an MVP of Sparky and is preparing to test it
> with real learners and Journey course creators. Now, we're looking for technology partners and
> sponsors to help take Sparky to the next level.

**The ask — named roles, not a vibe**

> We're seeking coders, data scientists, AI experts, LLM trainers, and corporate collaborators who
> want to build something revolutionary with us.
>
> Beyond technical assistance, we also welcome support through employee volunteer programs, pro bono
> services, internship pipelines, and financial sponsorships that can expand access to Journey courses
> and Sparky AI in more communities.

**Closer — the one crafted line, held until the very end**

> Let's work together to launch and scale Sparky AI—because learning is a journey, and everyone
> deserves a guide.

## Reusable phrases

- "small but mighty team"
- "underserved learners" (used here for the global out-of-school population; contrast with
  `voice-profile.md`'s community-facing terminology table, which prefers "participants" for people
  actually enrolled in a Cambio program — this source is describing a population Cambio hasn't yet
  reached, a distinct use)
- "personalize and credentialize learning"
- "learning is a journey, and everyone deserves a guide"
